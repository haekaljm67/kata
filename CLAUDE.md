# Kata, a language learning website

## What we're building
- Kata is an interactive language learning website (a website, not an app) for students at mixed levels.
- The interface is in English.
- Launch languages: English, Indonesian, Japanese. French, German, Mandarin and Dutch come later, so structure content so new languages can be added without rebuilding the site.
- Levels follow known frameworks: CEFR for English and Indonesian, JLPT for Japanese. Start with beginner content (A1 / N5).

## Features for the first version
- Flashcards with spaced repetition. One big word per card, tap to flip, two answer buttons (Missed / Know), optional romanization hint. A word counts as mastered only after it's answered correctly on two different days.
- Quizzes (multiple choice, listening, fill in the blank). Every answer gets a short explanation of why it's right or wrong.
- Writing practice with Guided and Free-form modes. Guided shows a start dot and arrow for each stroke. Japanese starts with a kana-first foundations path.
- Lesson path as a vertical list with locked lessons grayed out and a "Start here" marker on the next one.
- Optional placement test so learners can skip what they already know.
- Chat partner: scripted conversation practice for now, no paid AI API.
- "Word of the hour" card on the Today page: reading, meaning, an example sentence and audio.
- Progress: word milestones (10, 50, 100, 200, 500), a daily streak with weekday dots, and counts for learning / mastered / needs practice.
- Works without an account. Signing up saves progress (free option such as Supabase).

## Welcome page
- At /welcome. Content in order: a one- or two-sentence intro with the mascot; the three languages, each with one real sample word shown like a flashcard front (set by `sampleWord` in each language.json); what you can do, with small previews built from the real flashcard and quiz parts, and features that don't exist yet marked "Coming soon"; "How it works" in three steps; and a "Start learning" button to Today, with a note that no account is needed.
- Shown on a first visit that lands on Today: someone who has never tapped "Start learning" and has no saved progress. After "Start learning", visits go straight to Today. Direct links to other pages are never redirected.
- Always reachable at /welcome and from Settings → About → About Kata. No bottom navigation on this page.
- Written plainly, the way a person describes their own project. No gradient hero, no grid of icon cards, no hype words.

## Settings
- Its own page at /profile/settings, opened with a gear icon: in the top-right corner on phones and tablets (with a back arrow to Profile on the Settings page), and in the bottom-left corner of the sidebar on desktop. Profile also has a Settings row. Every choice is saved in the browser and applies immediately, with no Save button.
- Only add settings for features that actually exist. No switches for future features.
- Appearance: Theme (System default, Light, Dark), with no flash of the wrong theme on load and a browser bar color (theme-color) that matches. Text size (Default, Large). Animations (Match device default, or Off).
- Learning: Daily goal (5, 10 or 20 words a day, default 10), counted as different words practiced today in flashcards or quizzes. Reading help for romanization and kanji readings (Always, On tap default, Never). It applies to the question side of flashcards and quizzes; answers always show readings. Languages shown in Practice, with at least one always on.
- Audio: Autoplay a word when its flashcard appears (default off). Speech speed (Normal, Slow). Sound effects for answers (default off), generated by the browser with no audio files.
- Your data: Reset progress, with a confirmation step. Settings are kept.
- About: credits for words, audio, fonts and icons; the version number from package.json; and the line "Your progress is saved in this browser."

## Content and audio
- You write the lesson content. Keep it accurate and natural, and flag anything a native speaker should double-check.
- Audio: use free, properly licensed native recordings where they exist (e.g. Tatoeba, Lingua Libre) and credit them. Otherwise use the browser's built-in text-to-speech.

## Design
- Calm and premium, with a little warmth. It must not look cheap, templated, or like generic AI-made design. No ads, no clutter.
- Mobile-first. Bottom navigation on phones and tablets with four tabs: Today, Practice, Collection, Profile.
- Desktop (1024px and wider): a quiet left sidebar with the mascot logo and the same four sections replaces the bottom tabs. Page widths: one-task pages (Flashcards, Quiz, Settings) stay a narrow focused column; other pages get up to 768px; the welcome page uses side-by-side sections up to 1024px. Widths are set in one place, lib/navigation.ts.
- Light and dark mode, following the device setting by default.
- Reference: Hourly Kana (iOS app). Borrow its structure and clarity: big single words on clean cards, the practice hub, the lesson path, the progress screens. Don't copy its loud colors, its fonts, or its mascot.
- Sans-serif only. Plus Jakarta Sans for interface text, Zen Kaku Gothic New for Japanese. No serif fonts.
- Colors: off-white #F7F5F0, ink #1F2A2E, sage #7A9E8E, clay #C8795A (main accent for buttons and correct answers), line #E4E0D8
- A simple original mascot: a small rounded ink drop with dot eyes, in ink #1F2A2E with clay or sage accents. It doubles as the logo: small, with the word "Kata", in the top left of every page (in the sidebar on desktop), where it's the home button (goes to Today; on the welcome page it scrolls to the top). It's at least 44×44 px to tap, labeled "Kata home", and never covers content. Pages one level down (Flashcards, Quiz, Settings) show a back arrow there instead. Apart from the logo, the mascot is used sparingly: empty states, streaks, milestones, and the favicon.
